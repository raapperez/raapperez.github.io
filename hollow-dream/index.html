The Game

You wake up in a room you have never seen. You remember nothing. The door behind you is locked.

Hollow Dream is a first-person psychological horror game set inside a dream that won't let you go. Every door you open locks behind you. There is no going back, only deeper. Find batteries, keep the light for when it's dark, and keep going.

Coming soon to Android.

Features

  • A new house every time you fall asleep: rooms, corridors and doors built anew for every dream.
  • A flashlight, batteries that run out, and keys for the doors that won't open.
  • A story told across 24 pages, found over many dreams.
  • A retro, low-poly look inspired by classic horror games.
  • Plays offline, with touch or gamepad controls, in English or Portuguese.

Something in Here

The dream is not empty. Some things wait in the dark. Some only move when your back is turned. Listen to the floor, keep your light where it matters, and know when to run.

A Story in Pages

Notes are scattered through the dream, written in different hands. A hospital chart. A child's drawing. A letter that always ends with "M". Find them across your dreams and piece together why you are here, and what happened on that rainy Thursday.

  • The story comes in order. Every page you find is kept, so the next dream picks up where you left off.
  • The deeper you go, the more you find. The story moves faster further down.
  • Read the loose pages too. Some are advice from whoever was here before you.

Content Advisory

Contains frightening scenes and creatures, themes of death, injury and loss, sudden loud sounds and flickering lights. Intended for players aged 13 and over.
