Privacy Policy for Hollow Dream

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Hollow Dream ("the app", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you play the game. Hollow Dream is developed by Gryphon Entertainment (the "Developer").

Information We Collect

Hollow Dream does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, or other directly identifying information. The app uses the following third-party services, which may collect data as described below:

Google AdMob (Advertising)

The app shows a full-screen interstitial ad using Google AdMob, at most once each time the app is started (never on the first launch after installing), after a notice that an ad will play. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, and app usage data to serve and measure ads. Depending on your region and consent choice, ads may be personalized or non‑personalized. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Google User Messaging Platform (Consent)

We use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain your consent for personalized advertising where required by law (e.g. GDPR in the EEA/UK, and applicable US state privacy laws). The consent form is shown when the app starts, where applicable.

Firebase Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics (Google) to understand gameplay patterns and improve the app — for example, which screens are visited, how deep into the dream players get and what ended a run, and which language is used. This data is aggregated and not used to identify you personally. See Firebase's Privacy and Security page.

Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to automatically collect crash reports and diagnostic data (such as device model, OS version, and stack traces) when the app crashes or encounters an error. This helps us fix bugs and improve stability.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your settings, language, deepest depth reached, and the story pages you have found are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us, and they are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data described above is shared only with the third‑party service providers listed (Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics) for the purposes described, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Hollow Dream is a horror game intended for a mature audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Choices

  • You can decline personalized ad consent when prompted (where applicable by region).
  • You can reset or limit your device's advertising identifier in your device's system settings.
  • You can uninstall the app at any time to stop all data collection by it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
