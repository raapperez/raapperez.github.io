The Game

The Halden Building is a first-person psychological horror mystery. The city has flooded, and you have just taken the job of superintendent in an old apartment block. By day you keep the building running and listen to the tenants. By night, something in the building starts to move.

Every tenant has a story, an alibi and something to hide. Find out what happened in the Halden Building before the last night.

Coming soon to Android.

Features

  • Seven days and seven nights in a single building, floor by floor.
  • Twelve tenants to talk to, suspect and protect.
  • Several endings, with new options unlocked as you find them.
  • A retro, low-poly look inspired by classic horror games.
  • Plays fully offline, with touch or gamepad controls.

Finding the Endings

There are eight endings. Each one you find unlocks something new for your next run. A few tips, without spoilers:

  • Naming her isn't enough. The police need proof. Fill your journal before you pick up the lobby payphone.
  • Don't trust every clue. Some evidence was planted. Accuse the wrong woman and the police will believe you.
  • Protect the tenants. Justice tastes different when there is nobody left in the building to thank you.
  • Watch the clock in the basement. On the last night, what happens when time runs out depends on who cares about you, and who you cared about.
  • Listen to the old super. Abel left six cassette tapes hidden around the building. Find all of them before you make the call.
